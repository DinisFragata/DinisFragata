# Hi, I'm Dinis

I'm a developer from Sertã, in central Portugal. I build simple websites for local businesses and help them get their Google Business Profile in order. For companies and agencies I do AI automations and integrations (OpenAI, Google Sheets/Apps Script, APIs) and React/Next.js work.

Before that I did internships at Neural AI in Malta (9 client projects in AI and automation, Best Intern of the Month) and at Dilium in Milan (frontend on their production sites, with Svelte and Tailwind).

## Projects

### [Hotel.Operations](https://github.com/DinisFragata/Hotel.Operations)

<a href="https://www.dinisfragata.pt/projects/hotel-operations/demo">
  <img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bf971c07-0ea3-4c54-b923-cf50b6990117" alt="Hotel.Operations dashboard" width="100%">

A dashboard for the day-to-day of a small hotel team: reservations, rooms, guests, maintenance and check-in/check-out, plus an AI assistant that answers questions using the hotel's own data. For the AI ​​assistant, I had to add a code layer before the response was sent to the user, so that it could include charts and visual statistics.

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Prisma, PostgreSQL, Tailwind, Vercel AI SDK. [Try the demo](https://www.dinisfragata.pt/projects/hotel-operations/demo) (fictional data).

### [PDF Automation Report](https://github.com/DinisFragata/PDF-Automation-Report)

Upload a sales spreadsheet and get a PDF report with metrics and charts. I started it after seeing, during my internship, how much time people spend turning spreadsheets into reports by hand. The full version is Python + FastAPI; the [demo](https://dinisfragata.pt/projects/pdf-automation-report/demo) builds the PDF in the browser.

### [School management app](https://github.com/DinisFragata/Professional-Aptitude-Test-2024-2025) (final school project, 20/20)

A mobile app and a website for my school: attendance, grades, lunch tickets and logins by role. It took me 7 months. React Native, Expo, PHP and MySQL.
